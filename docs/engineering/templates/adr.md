> An ADR records a decision that **has been made** and its consequences.
> It is never used to record what has not been decided, and it never records a decision that the
> owner has reserved (technology, hosting, integrations - see project-context.md).

## Context

What situation forces a decision? What constraints apply? Which of the owner-stated constraints
(`C-1` .. `C-5`) are relevant? Keep it factual and short.

## Reversal cost

How expensive is it to undo this decision later, and what would have to change? If the reversal cost
is high, say so explicitly - it is the main reason this record exists.

## Verification

explicit statement that it is not directly verifiable).
